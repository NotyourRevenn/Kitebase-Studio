04 / Common Questions

Straight answers before reaching out.

What does a typical project cost?

Every build is quoted in writing before work starts. Focused business websites typically start at $500; ongoing social media, automation, and monthly reporting retainers start from $200/month.

Can I book just web design, or just AI & social?

Yes. Pick any individual service—like adding an appointment chatbot to an existing site—or combine web, automations, social management, and reporting into one setup.

How long does a website take?

Most focused business sites take 5–10 business days from kickoff to launch. Standalone automations or booking chatbots usually wrap in 3–5 business days.

How do time zones and revisions work?

Work runs aligned to US business hours—feedback sent during your day is shipped overnight. You review a live staging link first and request revisions within scope before going live.

Do I keep full ownership of everything?

Completely. Your domain, codebase, social handles, workflows, and customer data live under accounts you control.
