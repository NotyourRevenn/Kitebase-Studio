Questions, answered

What do you offer?

Website design, social media management, reporting and analysis, AI reels and videos, and AI automation and chatbots.

Who do you work with?

Small businesses in the United States that want to grow. We are based in India and schedule calls to suit your time zone.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted in writing before it starts. Pricing: Starting from $500 for a website, retainers from $200/month.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on scope, and you receive a delivery date in writing before work begins. Typical timeline: 5-10 business days for a website.

How will we communicate?

By email and short calls, with a written update after each step. Replies come within one business day.

What if I do not like the result?

You review a draft and can ask for changes before anything goes live. Revision terms: Unlimited revisions until you are happy, within scope.
